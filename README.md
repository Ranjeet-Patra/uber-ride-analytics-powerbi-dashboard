# uber-ride-analytics-powerbi-dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing ride funnel, conversion, cancellations and revenue insights

## Dashboard Link : https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/438a686d-a8d7-4949-ab85-5e8908a39414?ctid=56c1d497-700b-49cf-8f8d-3dd6b20d522f&pbi_source=linkShare

## Dashboard Demo Video : https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HW7eWH7rc-Z7cstplHHLXqiCzLIcVump/view?usp=drive_link

### 🔹 Overview & KPI Monitoring

* Designed a centralized Power BI dashboard to track core business KPIs including Completed Bookings, Lost Bookings, Revenue, Total Distance and Average Distance
* Enabled dynamic filtering by vehicle type and time (monthly & quarterly) for real-time performance tracking
* Provided a single source of truth for operational and financial metrics

### 🔹 Funnel & Conversion Analysis

* Analyzed the ride lifecycle (booking → completion/cancellation) to measure conversion rates and identify drop-offs
* Quantified lost bookings and cancellation trends to highlight inefficiencies in ride fulfillment
* Enabled data-driven insights into user drop-off behavior and conversion improvement opportunities

### 🔹 Revenue & Business Performance

* Built revenue analysis across vehicle type, customer segments and payment methods
* Performed monthly and quarterly trend analysis to identify growth patterns and seasonality
* Identified top-performing vehicle categories contributing to overall revenue

### 🔹 User Behavior & Segmentation

* Segmented users into First-time, Returning and Regular riders to analyze engagement patterns
* Analyzed cancellation reasons and payment preferences to understand customer behavior
* Delivered insights into usage trends for better decision-making

### 🔹 Location & Demand Intelligence

* Identified high-demand pickup/drop locations, busy time slots and peak ride areas
* Analyzed ride distribution and distance trends across vehicle types
* Supported operational planning using geospatial and time-based insights

### 🔹 Customer Experience Metrics

* Tracked average rider and driver ratings to monitor service quality
* Provided insights into customer satisfaction and experience trends

### 🔹 Dashboard Design & Interactivity

* Developed interactive dashboard with drill-downs, filters and multi-page navigation
* Implemented hide/show filter panel to improve usability and spacing
* Designed for easy exploration by business users without technical dependency

### 🚀 Business Impact

* Identifies key drivers of ride cancellations and lost revenue
* Enables optimization of pricing, vehicle allocation and operations
* Supports data-driven decision-making through real-time insights
* Improves understanding of user behavior and service performance
